Privacy policy

Effective: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how data is handled in Mangala & Matrak, published by Voty Apps.

No account

We never ask for your name, email, phone or location.

Progress stays with you

On your device and in your own iCloud/Google account.

Analytics by consent

Game statistics are sent only if you allow it.

No ads

We show no ads and never sell data.

There is no chat. Your opponent only sees your Game Center nickname. If you choose “Show my Game Center friends”, Apple asks for your permission; the list is used on your device only to show who you can invite and is never sent to us. Matching with random opponents is only available to accounts Apple reports as adult and without parental restrictions.

We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you for advertising. We do not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access. We have no servers of our own and store no personal data.

2. Consent

Sharing statistics is off by default. We ask when you first open the game and you can change your choice any time in Settings. Saying no never limits the game.

3. International transfers

Analytics data may be processed on servers of our provider Google (Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited) outside your country. iCloud and Game Center data is processed by Apple under its own responsibility. We never sell data or share it for advertising.

4. Retention

Statistics are deleted automatically after 2 months. Progress stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete the game or reset your progress.

5. Children

The game is designed for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn of it, we delete it. Random matchmaking is disabled for child accounts.

6. Your rights (UK GDPR / GDPR)

Core gameplay is based on performing our contract with you; statistics are based only on your consent. You have the right to access, correct, delete, object to and port your data, and to complain to your local supervisory authority (in the UK, the ICO). Contact us at destek@votyapps.com; we reply free of charge within 30 days.

7. United States

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Residents of US states with privacy laws (such as California) may request access to or deletion of their data at the address above.

8. Security and changes

Connections between the game and Google and Apple are encrypted. If we update this policy we will change the date above and announce important changes in the game.
