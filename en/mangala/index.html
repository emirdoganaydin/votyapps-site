How to play

Moves on the board, duels in the arena

1

Make your move

Pick up the stones in a pit and sow them one by one, counter-clockwise.

2

Fill your store

Every stone in your store powers your warrior; land the last stone there and you go again.

3

Strike

As stones land in your store, your warrior hits the opponent. More stones, harder blows!

Warriors

Choose your warrior

Each warrior has a fighting style of his own. Win matches to rise through the ranks.

Boards

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I play online?

If you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, tap “Play online” and invite a friend. Friends who don't have the game yet can get this page's link via “Invite a friend”.

Do I need an account?

No. We never ask for an email or password. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Is there chat in online matches?

No. There's no messaging, only preset reactions. Your opponent only sees your Game Center nickname.

What data do you collect?

We don't collect your name, email or advertising ID. To improve the game we count in-game events only with your permission, without identifying you. Details: Privacy policy.

Is there an Android version?

For now it's on iPhone and iPad. We're working on Android.
