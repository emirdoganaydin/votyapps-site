FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no in-app purchases.

Which platforms?

Coming soon to iOS and Android. On iPhone and iPad your progress follows you.

How do I play online?

Invite a friend to an online match (Game Center on iPhone). Friends without the game can get this page's link.

Is there chat in online matches?

No. There's no messaging, only preset reactions. Your opponent only sees your nickname.

What data do you collect?

No name, email or advertising ID. Statistics only with your consent and without identifying you. Details: Privacy policy.
