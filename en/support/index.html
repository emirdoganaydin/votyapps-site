Mangala & Matrak

Online play doesn't work

You need to be signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center). The friend you invite also needs the game and must be your Game Center friend. Playing the computer or two players on one phone works without signing in.

I can't see random matchmaking

Matching with random opponents is only available to accounts Apple reports as adult and without parental restrictions. You can always play with friends.

My progress doesn't show on my iPad

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices. Syncing can take a few minutes.

I don't want to share statistics

Turn off statistics sharing in the game's Settings. Nothing is sent from that moment on.

I want my data deleted

Reset your progress in the game's Settings. For anything else, email us.
