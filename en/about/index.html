About us

A small team, AI agents and fast experiments. Voty Apps is a mobile game studio where founder Emir Doğan Aydın works side by side with AI agents.

How we work

1

Discover

Our insight bot scans App Store and Google Play charts every day and reports risers and categories where young games can break in.

2

Challenge

Every big decision is written up and stress-tested by an independent AI playing a skeptical investor.

3

Build

Code, tests and builds are done with AI agents; iOS and Android versions are built in the cloud. Art is made with AI tools.

4

Measure

Games are measured only with consent and without identifying players. We grow what works and drop what doesn't.

Our principles
